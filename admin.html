Email

Resolved-ticket emails and assignment emails are sent from one Microsoft 365 mailbox. Send yourself a test to check it works; if it doesn't, this shows what Microsoft objected to.

IT ticket SLAs

Targets shown on IT → Requests → SLAs and on each ticket. A new ticket nobody has answered within the acknowledgement time gets one automatic "we got your ticket and we're working on it" email (only tickets that come in after this was switched on; never Excel imports).

HR global calendar

Shown as its own tab on the Calendar page (List or Month), and used to fill in "Who's in office" from entries like "Greg – PTO" or "Alexis WFH". Leadership logins only. In Outlook on the web: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → pick the calendar → choose "Can view titles and locations" (or all details — "busy only" has no names) → Publish → copy the ICS link. The link is kept on the server and never shown to other users, but anyone who has it can read the calendar, so don't share it elsewhere.

Quarterly review targets

Used by Target attainment on the Overview's quarterly review (Leadership). Procurement is measured on new entered sales orders; Purchasing on gross profit.

HR calendar colours

Entries on the HR calendar are coloured by the company of the person in them, and holidays / closures get their own colour. List people as their names appear on the HR calendar (commas or one per line) under their company, or under Global if they work across all four. Anyone not on a list counts by their login's company, or SOI. If a first name is at two companies (e.g. two Chrises), use full names.

Performance escalation

How many countable notes (factual, dated, and discussed with the employee — not serious misconduct) prompt a review for the next step, and how long notes stay live. These only flag a review; a step is always recorded by a person, and termination always needs HR and legal review. HR's numbers are placeholders — confirm them with HR.

Default person per department

A new task added in a department goes to this person when nobody else is picked — e.g. every IT request lands with IT. Managers can still assign anyone. Suggested picks are shown until you save.

Claude projects

Paste each department's Claude project link (it starts with https://claude.ai/project/). It shows up as a "Claude project" button on that department's tab and opens in a new tab — claude.ai can't be embedded inside the tracker. Leave blank for none.
